Tinkering Club: call for applications

November 10, 2025
Reading time: about 1 minute

APPLICATIONS STILL OPEN! STARTING NEW GROUPS ON A ROLLING BASIS…

‼️➡️ Tinkering Club application ⬅️‼️

“What’d you get done this week?”

If you’d like to answer that question weekly to a group of exceptional peers, this is for you.

I’m trying an experiment: building a cohort of 3-6 hand-picked tinkerers / hackers / builders / makers / artists. They can be working on software, hardware, design, carpentry, artistry, whatever.

Every week, each person shares a 2-5 minute Loom / demo of what they built. No weeks off (with reasonable exceptions). And each week, they watch everyone else’s.

100% free. Accountability & inspiration guaranteed or your money back.

The focus is on solo projects or those with 1-2 collaborators, but you can apply even if that’s not you — as long as you’ll be able to demo the work that you personally have done every week. Main focuses and side projects both happily accepted.
